苏哥的钓鱼竿 已就位 Su Ge's Video Rod is ready

刷到的视频和图，一竿收进你的私人素材库 Every video and image you scroll past, one cast from your own library

嗅探内核来自开源猫抓 cat-catch（GPL-3.0）；素材库、批量下载、命名归档与界面为本版重做。 Sniffing core from open-source cat-catch (GPL-3.0); the library, batch download, naming and UI are rebuilt in this build.

01

什么网页都能钓 Any page works

网页上出现的视频和图片，随手就能存下来；单张存、整页批量存都行。 Any video or image on the page — save one file or the whole batch.

02

存下来就入库 Saved means filed

自动进本地素材库：能搜、能预览、能筛选，素材不再散落在下载文件夹里。 Everything lands in a local library you can search, preview and filter.

03

命名归档按你的规矩 Naming on your rules

文件名模板自己定：标题、作者、序号、日期随便拼，按站点和作者自动分文件夹。 Your filename template, your folders — split by site and author.

04

只在你电脑里跑 Runs on your machine

全程本地处理，不上传、不跟踪、不用注册账号，素材始终是你的。 Fully local: no upload, no tracker, no account. The files stay yours.

隐私政策 Privacy PRIVACY

所有数据均在你本地存储与处理，不发送到任何远程服务器，不含任何跟踪器。 All data is stored and processed locally. Nothing is sent to a remote server, and no trackers are included.
